# Scoring rubric

Three axes, 1–5 each. Ported from the v1 QA rubric with the Design axis (pixels) replaced by Differentiation (audience judgment) — Suade does not score pixels.

## Content

Audience fit · message clarity · evidence grounding · correctness of each slide's role.

- **5** — every claim grounded, every slide's message stateable in one sentence, the room's top questions all answered where they arise.
- **4** — solid; a couple of partial-grounding slides or one soft role mismatch.
- **3** — the argument exists but several beats are asserted rather than proven.
- **2** — key claims uncited; roles confused (a traction slide doing market's job).
- **1** — content does not survive contact with the room.

## Coherence

Pacing across the deck · no redundant slides · consistent narrative arc · variation without chaos.

- **5** — every slide earns the next; the deck reads as inevitable.
- **4** — one unearned transition or one redundant beat.
- **3** — the spine is discernible but the order fights it in places.
- **2** — reads as sections stapled together.
- **1** — no arc; a pile of slides.

## Differentiation

Would this deck be meaningfully different for a different room? It should be. A deck that could be shown unchanged to YC and to a quarterly board is optimized for neither.

- **5** — opening, emphasis, and proof selection are visibly built for THIS room (per the lens).
- **4** — right general shape, some generic beats.
- **3** — a competent generic deck; the room's specific priorities are incidental.
- **2** — mismatched: leads with what this room discounts.
- **1** — wrong deck for the room.

## Acceptance (a deck that is "ready")

No hard fails · every axis ≥ 4 · average ≥ 4.2. State pass/fail against this bar in the verdict.

## Investor quick-lenses

Temporary home until the full a16z/sequoia/accel lens files get their critique edit; ported verbatim from the v1 review module. The yc-demo-day and board-quarterly lenses already carry their checklists in their own files — prefer those.

**a16z** — "Lead with the contrarian insight, then prove the moat behind it." Open with the non-obvious thesis or market reframe. Proof focus: contrarian insight, technical moat, why-now shift. Risk: commodity AI framing, generic growth claims with no defensible secret. Checklist: strong why-now and a secret worth believing? moat tied to product/data/distribution compounding? bold without drifting from evidence?

**sequoia** — "Show the bones of an enduring category-defining company." Open with clarity of purpose and a market that sustains long-term leadership. Proof focus: category scale, execution credibility, enduring-company signals. Risk: short-term hype without durable leadership path. Checklist: believable ten-year reason to exist? product quality and customer pull concrete? team capable of building an institution?

**accel** — "Center the product, PMF signals, and repeatable execution." Get to product or customer pull fast, then retention/engagement/GTM repeatability. Proof focus: PMF evidence, usage intensity, repeatable GTM. Risk: vanity metrics, high-concept vision before operational proof. Checklist: survives hard PMF questions? proof shows repeatability, not one-off wins? product tangible rather than narrated?
