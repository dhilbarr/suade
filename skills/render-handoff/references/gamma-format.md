# Gamma handoff format (`handoff/gamma.md`)

Gamma imports pasted text/markdown and splits it into cards. Make the split deterministic: one card per slide, separated by `---` on its own line.

Per card:

````markdown
# <slide title — the claim>

- <proof bullet from must_prove/evidence, max 4>
- <...>

<!-- visual: <visual_hint> -->
<!-- note: <core message>. Transition: <earns_next> -->
---
````

Rules:

- `#` heading per card; Gamma treats headings as card titles.
- Bullets carry evidence, not restatements of the title. A cover/hook card may have zero bullets — that is correct, not empty.
- HTML comments survive paste as invisible hints for the human editing in Gamma; Gamma itself ignores them. Do not put load-bearing content in comments.
- Gaps: a visible line `[GAP: retention data missing — do not invent]` on any card whose blueprint slide had `open_gaps`.
- Close the file with a plain paragraph: "Generated by Suade from <deck_title> blueprint v<version>, audience <lens>, <date>. Instructions: create new Gamma → Paste in text → paste everything above this line."

Keep total length lean — Gamma over-generates from long inputs. The blueprint's discipline (one idea per slide) is the constraint that keeps Gamma honest.
